Re-DevAfrica

Redeveloping Africa, one community at a time.

Re-DevAfrica is a non-profit organization dedicated to Africa's redevelopment — investing in education, health, economic opportunity, and sustainable infrastructure so that communities across the continent can build their own lasting future.

Our Mission

A continent redeveloped from within.

We believe Africa's redevelopment has to be led by the people who call it home. Our work is about removing the barriers that stand between African communities and the tools, skills, and infrastructure they need to thrive on their own terms — not aid that ends when the funding does, but capacity that outlasts it.

“Redevelopment isn’t something done to a community — it’s something built with one, and sustained by one.”
Where We Focus

Four pillars, one continent.

Real redevelopment touches every part of daily life. We organize our work around four areas where a real, durable difference is possible.

📚

Education & Skills

Expanding access to quality schooling and practical, market-relevant skills training so young people can build careers at home.

❤️

Healthcare Access

Supporting community health infrastructure and preventive care so a family's health is never the barrier to their future.

💰

Economic Empowerment

Backing local entrepreneurs and small businesses with the capital, mentorship, and market access to grow sustainably.

🏗️

Sustainable Infrastructure

Investing in clean energy, clean water, and digital connectivity built to last generations, not just election cycles.

How We Work

Principles we hold ourselves to.

1

Community-led

Every initiative starts with the community it serves, not a plan handed down from outside it.

2

Partnership-driven

We work alongside local organizations, institutions, and leaders already doing the work on the ground.

3

Built to last

We measure success by what keeps running after we've stepped back, not by what looks good on day one.

4

Transparent

Where our support goes and what it accomplishes should always be a straightforward, honest answer.
